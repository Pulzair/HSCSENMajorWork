How to play

The basics

What the game is and what a player should aim to do.

Turns

Everyone gives orders at the same time and then they resolve. If you enable a timer, the timer will grow with empire size.

The three layers

Land, underground and sky, with crossing points that allow unit travel between layers.

Giving orders

Click one of your units to select it, then click a highlighted tile to move or attack. Click one of your cities to open its production panel. Double click a tile to switch between unit and production.
